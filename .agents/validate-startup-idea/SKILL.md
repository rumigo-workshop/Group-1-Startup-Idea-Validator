# Startup Idea Validation Workflow

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Problem & ICP:** Identify the exact target customer and verify pain severity.
2. **Market Sizing:** Estimate TAM, SAM, and SOM using bottom-up calculations.
3. **Competitor Matrix:** List top 3 direct alternatives and identify the unfair advantage.

## Output Rules

- Present scores in a 1–10 rubric table.
- Flag fatal regulatory or unit-economics risks upfront.
